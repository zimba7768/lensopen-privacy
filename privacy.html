LensOpen Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

LensOpen is a camera app developed by Seabell. This page explains what information the app accesses and how it is used.

Camera and photos

LensOpen uses your device's camera to take photos, including long exposure shots. Photos you capture are saved directly to your device's storage, either in the app's own folder or a folder you choose. LensOpen does not upload your photos anywhere. They stay on your device unless you share them yourself.

Permissions

The app requests camera access to take photos, and storage access to save them to a folder of your choosing. It does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, or any other device data.

Purchases

LensOpen offers an optional one time in-app purchase, the Pro Upgrade, handled entirely through Google Play Billing. LensOpen does not see or store your payment information. Google's own privacy policy governs that transaction: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data collection

LensOpen does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. There are no analytics, no ad tracking, and no account sign-in required to use the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to seabell.dev@gmail.com.
