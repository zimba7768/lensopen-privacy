LensOpen Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

LensOpen is a camera app developed by Seabell. This page explains what information the app accesses and how it is used.

Camera and photos

LensOpen uses your device's camera to take photos, including long exposure shots. Photos you capture are saved directly to your device's storage, either in the app's own folder or a folder you choose. LensOpen does not upload your photos anywhere. They stay on your device unless you share them yourself.

Permissions

The app requests camera access to take photos. It saves photos with the Android media system or to a folder you choose, so it does not need storage permission. It requests location access only if you turn on the Location option in Settings, which is off by default. It does not request access to your contacts, microphone, or any other device data.

Location

The Location option is off by default. If you turn it on, Android asks for your permission. While the app is open, LensOpen reads your device's location and writes the place into the photo file when you save a photo, in the same way a standard camera app does. LensOpen keeps your location only in the device's memory while the app is open. It does not store a location history, and it does not send your location to the developer or to anyone else. Anyone you share a photo file with sees the place saved in that file, so turn the option off before you share photos taken at home. You turn the option off in Settings, and you remove the permission in Android's app settings at any time.

Purchases

LensOpen offers an optional one time in-app purchase, the Pro Upgrade, handled entirely through Google Play Billing. LensOpen does not see or store your payment information. Google's own privacy policy governs that transaction: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data collection

LensOpen does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. There are no analytics, no ad tracking, and no account sign-in required to use the app. The in-app log file stays on your device. It holds camera information and error messages but no photos and no location coordinates, and LensOpen shares the log only when you choose Share log.
